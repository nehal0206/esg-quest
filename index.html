Why This Matters for Equity Researchers

ESG is no longer a checkbox — it's a capital allocation signal. SEBI BRSR mandates quantified disclosure for top-1000 listed companies. Higher ESG scores correlate with 27.8% ROCE for Leaders vs 7.4% for Weak companies (Crisil 2025). This simulation lets you experience the trade-offs an operator faces — the same ones that show up in EBITDA margins, capex cycles, and cost of debt.
